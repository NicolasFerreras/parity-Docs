# Value Proposition — Parity

> Public document. Claim, problem→solution→benefit and differentiator

## 1. Claim

> **Messy order in, validated order out. No re-typing, no hidden doubts.**

## 2. Problem → Solution → Benefit

| | Content |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | Every customer sends orders their own way: WhatsApp, mail, PDF, photo. Translating by hand costs hours and adds errors. |
| **Solution** | parity reads any format, crosses it with your catalog and leaves it in a familiar table, flagging what needs your eye. |
| **Benefit** | Hours back, errors avoided, control intact. One-click export, with traceability. |
| **Differentiator** | It doesn't replace your judgment. It amplifies it. It flags the ambiguous instead of inventing — and learns from each correction. |

## 3. Who it hurts and how much

- Much of the workday goes to validation (tens of minutes per order).
- Top qualitative frustration: duplicates ("manual re-translation"); most frequent symptom: ambiguity.
- The pain crosses roles: admin, sales, logistics and purchasing.

## 4. Why we win

Evaluated against 4 project alternatives, Parity won on *problem fit* and *development time*: it attacks exactly the 4 symptoms with the least complexity.
